NALA is a fintech building cross-border payments infrastructure for emerging markets. Its consumer app is an international money-transfer and multi-currency account used to send money from the UK, US and EU to 35+ countries across Africa and Asia, with 98% of transfers arriving within minutes. NALA's B2B product, Rafiki, is a single API for global payouts and collections: move, collect and settle funds in local currency or stablecoins (USDC/USDT) across the US, UK, Europe, Asia and Africa, reaching 300+ banks and 40+ mobile-money operators with real-time FX, lookups, wallet statements, webhooks and built-in compliance and sanctions screening. Backed by Bessemer Venture Partners.

**Areas:** Company · Fintech · Payments · Remittances · Money Transfer · Payouts · Cross-Border Payments · Mobile Money · Stablecoins · Africa

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for NALA: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
